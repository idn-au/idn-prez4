Description

The Wickham Motorcross Indigenous Land Use Agreement (ILUA) is a body corporate agreement involving the Minister for Lands, Ngarluma Aboriginal Corporation and the Shire of Roebourne. The agreement concerns a reserve south of Wickham for a motorcross track. This is an abridged display of the preserved public ATNS description; the source record and full body text remain available.

Page anatomy

Design notes only — not part of the production page.

  1. One identityThe preserved ATNS resource IRI remains the only root IRI. No second policy page, duplicate root title, or redundant odrl:uid is introduced.
  2. Two RDF typesShow schema:CreativeWork and odrl:Agreement together only after ODRL enrichment passes its publication gate; retain atns:Entity and the soft Agreement type.
  3. Preserved record firstPresent the canonical schema:name, schema:description, source identifier, access status, references and relationships without implying they came from ODRL.
  4. Interpretation boundaryPlace reviewed ODRL statements in a visually separate, clearly qualified card. Provenance and review status must distinguish them from recovered source assertions.
  5. Rules, not just PermissionsRender each odrl:permission, odrl:prohibition or standalone odrl:obligation as its own named card. A Duty nested under a Permission has a different meaning.
  6. Reciprocal rolesWhen justified, display one Party Collection as both assigner and assignee, with its reviewed members and an incompleteness warning. Prefer directional Rules where clause-level direction is known.
  7. Spatial distinctionShow the CreativeWork’s schema:spatialCoverage independently of a Rule’s odrl:target. Fetch a map on demand and keep a link to the full feature record.
  8. Profile work remainsThis mockup is not the deployed Prez profile. A future combined-resource profile must select nested Rules, Action labels, Parties and Targets; Prez profile precedence for the multiple RDF types needs testing.

Illustrative rule and Party Collection IRIs are placeholders, not published resources. The proposed action vocabulary under example.org is also unpublished. If evidence does not support an ODRL Rule, omit the entire ODRL section and do not type the record as an ODRL Agreement. Missing optional values should remove the row; non-spatial Targets remain ordinary links.
